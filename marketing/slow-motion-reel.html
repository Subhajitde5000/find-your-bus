How to create slow motion Reel on your computer in 2 minutes

  1. Run on computer: Open marketing/slow-motion-reel.html directly (double-click) → Press F11 for fullscreen (hides browser bars).
  2. Record: Windows: Press Win + G → Click Record (XBox Game Bar) → Play one Reel above → Stop after 10 sec. Mac: Press Cmd + Shift + 5 → Select window → Record.
  3. Make it SLOWER: Import to CapCut Desktop (free) → Select clip → Speed → 0.7x → On “Set on Bus” tap, split clip and set that 1 sec to 0.5x for punch-in effect.
  4. Add audio: In Instagram Reels → Add Music → Search “slowed + reverb”, “Bengali lofi slowed”, or “Aesthetic slowed” → Align beat to tap moments.
  5. Export 9:16: CapCut → Ratio 9:16 → Export 1080×1920 30fps → Upload to IG Reel + YT Shorts + FB Reel. Caption from captions-bank.md.
  6. Pro slow-mo trick: Duplicate clip → Middle copy at 0.25x + add Motion Blur effect → Gives that cinematic “phone sharp, background blurred” feeling.

We already slowed these Reels to 0.3x — just recording gives you instant slow motion without extra editing. For even slower, follow Step 3. All Reels use real KonBus data — viewers get the exact experience.
